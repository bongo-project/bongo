| B13 | **(research PRD) Backup that scales past a few GB.** Operator raised 2026-08-22: the current backup does not survive hundreds/thousands of accounts. Correct, and it is THREE problems. (1) **Duplication** — `backup-extras.sh` tars everything nightly, but blobs are content-addressed and immutable, so it re-copies all historical mail to capture one day of new mail. (2) **Downtime stops being free** — measured 2026-08-22: 11.6s stop->start, entirely graceful shutdown, because tar of ~26MB is instant. gzip runs ~50MB/s, so past roughly 1GB the window becomes tar-bound and grows linearly; at 100GB it is an hour of nightly downtime.
